# Toy Story Racer

Toy Story Racer Archipelago randomizes progression across the 200 challenges of the NTSC-U PlayStation version of Toy Story Racer.

## Items

The main progression items are:

- Soldiers
- Rex
- Hamm
- Slinky
- Mr. Potato Head
- LGM
- Rocky
- Lenny
- Babyface

Soldiers unlock challenges according to the normal Toy Story Racer progression requirements.

The four starting characters are:

- Woody
- Buzz
- RC
- Bo Peep

## Locations

Locations include:

- 200 individual challenges
- 12 Tower Complete checks
- 16 First Challenge Type checks

## Goals

Available goals are:

- One Tower
- Three Towers
- Six Towers
- All Towers

Complete the selected number of character towers to finish the Archipelago goal.
